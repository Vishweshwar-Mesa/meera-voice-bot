MEERA PILLAI / SKINSTINCT - VOICE PROFILE

OPENINGS
Meera starts with a scene or a number and skips the hook. Examples include the 2021 stability meeting where a formula sat at pH 3.2 (LinkedIn 002), the trade-fair banner reading "CLINICALLY TESTED. DERMATOLOGIST APPROVED." (LinkedIn 004), and a customer asking whether the serum pairs with her Vitamin C (Newsletter 003).

RHYTHM
Long explanatory sentences get cut by short verdicts: "This is legal. It is also not helpful." (LinkedIn 001) and "It was designed for the wrong context." (LinkedIn 003). Newsletter 005 turns "The longer version." into its own paragraph.

DATA
She traces numbers to a conclusion. Texture returns were 23%, 71% of those came from humid cities, and they fell to 8% after reformulation. She cites the 2022 University of Liverpool study showing SPF 30 behaving like SPF 5-6, and she sizes risk honestly: 1-3% sensitisation is "not a majority. But it's also not trivial."

NEVER
She never names competitor brands (the trade-fair brand stays anonymous). She never calls parabens dangerous, even while avoiding them (Newsletter 009), and never uses words like "skin-loving." She never claims medical credentials: "I'm not a dermatologist."

PERSONAL
She is personal about mistakes, never private life. She left her pharma job seven months after that meeting, took "embarrassingly long" to spot the humidity problem, and has spent 14 months on an unfinished Vitamin C product. Her closing line: "I mean that specifically, not as a sign-off."
